# Editing the Tavis website

You change the blog, the class timetable and the public holidays in the admin at **tavis.live/_emdash/admin**. Everything else on the site is changed by Cynco; ask us.

## How a change reaches the site

Every change starts as a draft that only you can see. **Publish** puts it on the site. The site takes about **five minutes** to update after you publish, even though the confirmation says visitors see it immediately. If a change still isn't there after ten minutes, tell Cynco.

The admin saves your work as you type, so you can close the page and come back to a draft later.

## Write a blog post

1. Open **Blog posts** and select **Add New**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Article**. Type `/` in the article for headings, pictures, lists and quotes.
3. The **Summary** shows under the title on the blog page and in Google results. Keep it to two sentences.
4. Add a **Cover picture**. It goes at the top of the post and on the blog page. Leave the **Blog page picture** empty unless the blog page needs a different picture.
5. Select **Publish**. To publish later, select **Schedule** instead and choose the date and time, in Malaysian time.

To change a published post, open it, make the change and select **Publish changes**. To take a post off the site, select **Unpublish**.

## Change the timetable

1. Open **Timetable** and select the form, for example **Form 4**.
2. Each weekday lists its classes. To change a class, edit its subject, tutor or times. To add a class, select **Add Item** under the day. To remove one, select the bin beside it.
3. Select **Publish changes**.

The order of the classes doesn't matter: the site sorts each day by start time. Subjects and times are chosen from a list. If you need a subject or a time that isn't there, ask Cynco.

## Add a public holiday

1. Open **Public holidays** and select **Add New**.
2. Type the holiday's name in Malay, for example `Hari Raya Aidilfitri`, and pick the date. Leave the time at 12:00 am.
3. Select **Publish**.

The timetable shows a holiday under its weekday. Holidays on a Saturday or Sunday don't show. Delete last year's holidays when you add the new year's.

## Signing in

You sign in with a passkey: your phone's or computer's fingerprint, face or PIN. An admin invites you from **Users**. Every person signs in as themselves; don't share an account.
